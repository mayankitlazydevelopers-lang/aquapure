Frequently asked questions

How often do filters need replacing?

Sediment and carbon filters last about 6 months, the RO membrane 2 to 3 years. The smart display alerts you before each change is due.

Does AquaPure remove essential minerals?

The Aurora and Aqua Mini add calcium and magnesium back after purification, so the water stays balanced and tastes natural.

Is installation included?

Yes. A certified technician installs your purifier free of charge, usually within 48 hours of delivery.

Which model suits my water source?

All models handle municipal, borewell and tanker water. For very high TDS (above 1,500 ppm), we recommend the Aurora.

What does the warranty cover?

Parts and labour for manufacturing defects. Filters and membranes are consumables and are covered for premature failure only.
